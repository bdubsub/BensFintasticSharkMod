**Client verification:** On the laptop, final multiplayer fixtures prove actual right click, boat and jump input, full body and fin presentation, Zippy day and darkness, algae animation, dive UV and movement pose, oxygen feedback and client receipt. The required client proof passes before merge. Missing laptop evidence leaves the relevant claim unverified and blocks P007-TASK-005.  
**Client audio isolation:** Before launch, zero master output in only the isolated Minecraft 1.20.1 instance. After launch, establish a verified owned Hyprland identity from `hyprctl clients -j` using exact address, class, title and PID, correlate that process tree to its PipeWire or PulseAudio application stream, apply a verified application mute with `wpctl` or `pactl`, and read back muted state before acceptance actions. Replacement stream handling reidentifies and remutes a recreated stream after reload, reconnect, device change or recreation. Teardown stops any watcher, removes temporary routing and isolated audio state, verifies the stream and process ended, and never changes a default sink, microphone, unrelated application or personal instance. Stop the owned client if identity or mute cannot be proven.  
**Budgets and privacy:** Capture is default off, at most 32 targets, 36000 ticks, 30 minutes wall time, 8192 queued records, 100000 emitted records, 64 MiB per capture and 256 MiB directory total, using stricter existing bounds when present. Movement samples at most once per target every five ticks except an eight target, 200 tick high detail reproduction. Pseudonyms replace identities. No private endpoint, NBT, chat, credentials or automatic upload is retained.
