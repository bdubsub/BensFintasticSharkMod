## Current implementation findings

**F005, OBSERVED.** The approved source already has `BfsDebugCommands`, `BfsDebugManager`, `BfsClientDebugManager`, `FishingCatchTrace`, `tools/bfs_debug_analyze.py`, and diagnostic GameTests. Server commands are `/bfs debug on`, `on <category> <ticks> [targets]`, `off`, and `status`, under permission level 2. Categories are all, movement, brain, combat, population, advancement, algae. One server session selects at most 32 entities, lasts 20 through 36,000 ticks, defaults to 1,200 ticks, and has a wall deadline, queue, record, session and directory bounds. Output is `bfs-debug-v2`; incomplete capture never passes. Existing client capture is local and separate. Extend these contracts and parser rather than create another capture subsystem. Sources: SRC-102, SRC-110. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-002 through BFS2-REQ-009, BFS2-REQ-022.

**F006, OBSERVED.** Current motion ownership is already substantially more developed than the historical anchor. `PitchSwimmingMoveControl` owns routes, powered carry, body pitch, finite clearance legs, settling, retries and speed limits. `SharkSwimmingMoveControl` delegates affected sharks to it. `AquaticMovement` defines fixed fish vertical ratio 0.20 and shark ratio 0.25. `AbstractSharkEntity.swimInWater` also supplies chase acceleration, bite braking, horizontal cap and speed floor. Other swimmers use distinct movement paths. All 22 species therefore require a capability and adapter inventory, not a change only to the shark movement attribute. Source: SRC-102. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-003, BFS2-REQ-004, BFS2-REQ-010.

**F007, INFERRED.** Merely adding two command values without revising the effective motion contract would leave vertical tuning suppressed by fixed ratio, route caps, current body projection and speed floor. Independently tunable horizontal and vertical values must reach the final powered motion integrator once each. The controller must then derive a safe body attitude and route compatible with that anisotropic velocity, rather than applying a second arbitrary vertical impulse after collision. Preserve steering, arrival, recovery, external impulse accounting and body safety; do not preserve the historical fixed vertical ratios as nonoverridable acceptance limits after the owner explicitly requested independent axes. Sources: SRC-102, SRC-110. Prerequisite: F006. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-003, BFS2-REQ-004, BFS2-REQ-010.

**F008, OBSERVED and INFERRED.** `PitchSwimmingMoveControl.clearSegment` checks translated axis aligned bounds for solid collision but tests water only at each sample's center. It does not check the full pitched visual body water envelope. `AbstractSharkEntity.onSharkTick` follows a predicted prey center, and beach avoidance runs only without an active target. This supports a credible route by which an upward chase approaches the surface with part of a long body out of water, then loses the water motion branch. It does not prove the report's exact reproduction. AABB collision alone cannot prove that a rotated long mesh avoids ground clipping. Source: SRC-102. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-010, BFS2-REQ-012.

**F009, OBSERVED.** Existing scale is synchronized `BfsScale` in `BfsAquaticEntity`, sampled on spawn and persisted to NBT, with `getDimensions` scaling and renderer scale calls. It is not universally inherited: stingray and seal extend `SmartWaterAnimal`, while Atlantic fish extend vanilla fish classes. A universal physical scale control needs complete 22 species support and matched dimensions/rendering, not a cast to `BfsAquaticEntity`. Spawn group quantity is a separate `speciesGroupMin` config path, currently populated for only selected species. Natural caps, spacing, vanilla replacement, administrative spawns and group extras are separate mechanisms in `MobCapManager` and `BfsSpawnPlacements`. Source: SRC-106. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-005, BFS2-REQ-006.

**F010, OBSERVED and INFERRED.** `BfsConfig` has existing spawn caps, chance, HP, damage, knockback, group minimum, disturbance and detection controls. `BensFintasticSharksForge.onEntityJoin` multiplies current base health and damage values and restores full health for a nondefault health multiplier. Repeated joins of persisted entities can therefore compound values or heal them. This is an important lifecycle risk for comprehensive live tuning. Use immutable base definitions plus idempotent named modifiers or absolute recomputation, with explicit health preservation semantics, and test unload, reload and repeated application. Source: SRC-106. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-002, BFS2-REQ-006.

**F011, OBSERVED and INFERRED.** Disturbance listeners presently cover sprinting while in water, attack, damage, underwater block break, a fall event requiring distance at least three and `isInWater`, and projectile impact. There is no dedicated boat movement or water jump transition producer. The player tick early exits out of water, so a seated boat rider is not covered by that path. `WaterDisturbanceHandler` converts the source to `LivingEntity`, which discards boat identity. Existing throttles use entity age and static maps; expiry and level separation need attention when adding frequent boat events. Source: SRC-102. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-007, BFS2-REQ-011, BFS2-REQ-012.

**F012, OBSERVED and INFERRED.** Zippy's base texture and glowmask both visibly contain authored pale cyan lightning markings. `CommonThresherRenderer` already registers `ConditionalAutoGlowingLayer`; `CommonThresherSharkEntity.hasGlowingLayer` returns true only below local raw brightness eight. Exact GeckoLib 4.4.7 source uses `_glowmask`, so the existing filename is correct. Its `AutoGlowingTexture.loadTexture` calls `GeoGlowingTextureMeta.createImageMask`, which transfers selected pixels and clears them from the base image before upload. Once that extraction happens, skipping the glow layer in bright light can remove the markings rather than render them normally. This is a strong static mechanism, not runtime proof. Prefer a nondestructive explicit overlay or preservation of the extracted markings in a normal light pass, keeping existing art, skin and dark glow. Do not invent a new animated energy effect. Sources: SRC-107, SRC-109. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-013.

**F013, OBSERVED.** All three algae IDs still instantiate a private `SeagrassBlock` subclass, with bonemeal disabled, and generated models use `minecraft:block/cross`. `AlgaePatchFeature` places one valid source water cell, searches down 16 and up four, preserves occupied blocks, and has no exposed seabed or cave exclusion. Placement uses rarity one in three, random XZ and uniform Y20 through Y62 across tagged ocean biomes. An ocean biome can contain a submerged cave, so the biome predicate alone does not exclude it. Large green and red textures already have discrete animation metadata with ten and nine frames respectively, each four ticks and no interpolation. Sources: SRC-107, SRC-102. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-014 through BFS2-REQ-016.

**F014, OBSERVED.** No dive armor is present in the inspected registration and armor paths. Prismarine armor uses a GeckoLib item/model/renderer contract and must remain a regression constraint. The initial four images are item icons. The owner subsequently supplied a 1280 by 1280 JPEG worn texture atlas, recorded in SRC-009; no geometry file was supplied. Exact Forge 47.2.0 sources provide `LivingBreatheEvent`, `PlayerEvent.BreakSpeed`, `PlayerInteractEvent.EntityInteractSpecific` and `EntityInteract`, equipment change and player lifecycle events, and gravity/swim attributes. Gravity adjustment does not bypass vanilla's water travel branch or prevent fluid jump input. Sources: SRC-102, SRC-108. Requirements: BFS2-REQ-017 through BFS2-REQ-019.

**F015, INFERRED.** Species policy is a plausible performance contributor beyond navigation: `SpeciesBehaviorEngine` performs bounded living scans, creates result lists, searches food blocks, and assigns feed routes. Stingray contact sting separately damages intersecting mobs. Routing lobsters and stingrays toward one another can increase contact deaths and replenishment cost. The code is consistent with the observed death pattern, but profiling must distinguish causal CPU work. `clearExit` can simulate up to 400 ticks for candidate safe exits, with repeated segment collision checks. It is another plausible expensive path and requires profiling rather than speculative removal. Source: SRC-102 and SRC-105. Requirement: BFS2-REQ-020.

## Architecture and alternatives

### Effective tuning and independent axes

Recommended: one server owned effective species settings service, keyed by full BFS registry ID, consumed by existing species adapters, commands, persistence and diagnostics. Keep immutable defaults, parsed validated profile data and temporary overrides distinct. Resolve precedence once and expose it in `get`/`list`; never read disk or parse registry strings each tick. Apply wildcard changes as one transaction after validating every affected field. Validate finite values, range ordering, nonnegative durations, upper resource bounds and safety constraints. Keep unavailable capabilities explicit rather than silently claim a setting changed a species that ignores it.

Use command names the owner supplied. A coherent surface is `/bfs debug setspeed <species|*> <horizontal> <vertical>` and `/bfs debug setsprint <species|*> <horizontalMultiplier> <verticalMultiplier>`, alongside `setspawnsize`, `setscale`, category specific settings, `get`, `list`, `reset`, and profile operations if selected. The exact unit choice is a consequential interface decision for the master, recommended blocks per second for base speed, unitless multipliers, blocks for range, ticks and human readable seconds for timing. Report target speed versus actual displacement separately. Inputs such as 12 and 100 are syntax illustrations, not desired defaults or compulsory accepted extremes.

Independent axis coefficients and body aligned steering are compatible when the controller constructs the intended anisotropic vector and steers safely toward its corresponding attitude. They are not compatible with blindly keeping the old fixed vertical ratio as a second ceiling. Preserve a clear distinction between configured desired speed and temporary collision/turn/surface limits. When blocked, show the limiting reason. Do not add a guarantee that every value is attainable against terrain or at every pitch. The default profile should start from current behavior; Ben owns subjective final tuning.

Alternative of setting only `MOVEMENT_SPEED` is inadequate because it cannot express independent axes and bypasses several bespoke paths. Duplicating tuning in each entity is also undesirable because wildcard application, reload, profile export and future fixes would drift. Existing species profiles provide the catalogue, but their locomotion descriptions should not be overloaded with live mutable state.

For attributes, named transient modifiers with stable IDs avoid multiplication on each join. Absolute recomputation is also viable if immutable base and previous health fraction are explicit. Either design needs lifecycle tests and must preserve other mods' modifiers.

### Following stick and temporary motion ownership

Use the existing command permission level and require a real player for giving the stick. Mark a vanilla stick with a versioned namespace NBT marker and identifiable name/lore. NBT is an identifier, not authorization: validate permission, item, hand, distance, live mob, level and ownership on every interaction. Handle the specific/general entity interaction sequence once; do not also tame, trade with, ride or damage the clicked target.

Represent following as a temporary owner UUID and selected mob UUID lease with bounded range, target count, timeout and route retry rate. Do not hold permanent entity references across unload. Ordinary goals, vanilla brains and SmartBrainLib require compatible adapters; preserve breathing and collision safety. Incompatible special bosses or third party controllers need the owner chosen scope and an explicit result, not a claim that any `Mob` can accept `TemptGoal`. A standard goal injection alone does not control every brain driven mob. On stop, only clear the walk intent or goal owned by this lease, then allow normal selection to resume. Repeated click behavior, another operator claiming a mob, loss of stick, death, logout, dimension change, mob unload and server shutdown all need declared deterministic behavior.

Recommended default is one follower per owner with explicit release/reselection. More followers are useful for group testing only if bounded and made visible. Do not automatically teleport mobs through walls or dimensions to satisfy following. Such teleportation would invalidate movement verification.

### Surface safety, disturbances and boat tracking

Add a shared body envelope query used by route selection, proposed pitch changes and recovery. Include entity scale, mesh body length, pivot, belly and dorsal fin distinction. A conservative sampled oriented hull is more faithful than entity center water alone; a larger axis aligned envelope is simpler but may incorrectly reject narrow valid routes. The master should select bounded sampling with species dimensions calibrated against existing authored geometry, and ensure no dynamic full mesh analysis runs in the hot path.

Observe actual water surface around the body instead of assuming global sea level. Fin only boat tracking needs a distinct surface locomotion intent, not a living combat target pointed at the rider or boat center. Select a safe waypoint behind the boat's travel direction with capped lead and turn rate, safe depth and bounded reacquisition. Prioritize actual survival and collision recovery above chasing. In insufficient water, drop or defer the surface intent and move toward safe water; do not force fin exposure through terrain. Boat removal, stationary boat, dismount, abrupt turn, shore approach, multiple boats and player dimension change require explicit outcomes. Preserve player hostility policy and do not attack boats.

Use actual jump/water transitions and boat movement at bounded intervals. A fall callback is not sufficient proof for a shallow jump splash. Track world tick, dimension and source identity for throttle state, prune on lifecycle or expiry, and carry source kind plus optional boat identity into the handler. Avoid a global scan of every entity each tick. Diagnostics should distinguish event absent, throttled, outside range, rejected by cooldown, accepted curiosity, acquired boat and unsafe surface route.

### Algae

Split small attachment algae from large column algae while preserving existing public registry IDs. A multiface waterlogged block, restricted to side and top supported faces, suits the requested glow lichen placement. Do not copy dry cave spreading just because glow lichen supplies geometry/state patterns. Preserve water and one appropriate loot result, including multiple faces in one cell.

Large algae can reuse the vanilla growing plant head/body pattern or use one block with explicit segment state. Head/body blocks follow kelp behavior more closely but require an internal registered body ID, item mapping and migration rules. A single stateful block preserves one ID but requires careful top detection, age, drops, break propagation and manual stacking. Whichever is selected, old single block placements must remain valid, support failure must restore water and drop only intended items, and resource generation must own blockstates/models/loot/tags.

Natural generation should choose the exposed ocean floor, require an unbroken suitable water column to the surface for red algae cave exclusion, preserve occupied structures and waterlogged blocks, and choose bounded column height limited by depth. Do not conflate natural generation restrictions with a ban on player decoration inside caves. Increase density using bounded patch counts/coverage and height distributions against the current sparse baseline. Existing animations already provide movement; no ecosystem, fish attraction, lighting or new particles are authorized merely by “give it life.” Random tick growth and bonemeal policy must be explicitly specified as part of kelp like stacking/growth rather than silently attached as unrelated extras.

### Dive equipment

Separate inventory item art from worn rendering. Reuse the established registry/platform structure while isolating client rendering. Never use the supplied item icons as if they were completed armor UV sheets. Armor material, durability/defense, equip sound, item naming, repair behavior and creative availability need a consistent approved choice; no crafting recipes means no generated recipes, not an inaccessible item.

A player owned oxygen reservoir is preferable to item NBT for preventing refill by swapping helmets or suits. Store remaining ticks with a schema, enforce 6,000 protected submerged simulation ticks for a full charge, decrement once on the authoritative server, refill only under the selected real air condition, and synchronize meaningful state changes plus bounded updates to the client. A HUD or vanilla air mapping must show capacity and empty state without pretending the vanilla 300 tick air store is the whole reservoir. Reequip, disconnect, restart and dimension transfer must not replenish underwater. Decide death and replacement suit behavior in the same lifecycle contract. Water Breathing, Respiration, creative, spectator, bubble columns and other equipment modifiers need explicit compatibility handling rather than accidental stacking.

Use Forge events for oxygen, equipment state, lifecycle and block break adjustment. Remove only actual water/airborne work penalties; do not grant correct tool harvest, remove Mining Fatigue, bypass protected blocks or change placement permissions. Test identical blocks, tools, effects and enchantments on land and submerged, grounded and jumping.

For walking, gravity and swim attributes alone are insufficient because the Forge 47.2.0 patched vanilla movement method still takes its water branch. Schedule an early concrete compatibility experiment before dependent final implementation: full suit eligibility, water contact, vanilla input, swim pose and airborne jump are observed on real server/client paths; determine whether scoped Forge events plus the existing movement hooks can achieve exactly one movement writer. If they cannot, document the missing hook and use the narrowest player only injection supported by the project instead of globally changing water or faking `onGround`. The experiment must prove no double travel, no lateral loss, no swim ascent, smooth jump apex/landing, normal dry travel, immediate partial suit exit and clean reconnect. A technical hypothesis is not an excuse to defer the phase's final implementation task.
